# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Target Personas
## AI Product Factory — Session 03 — Document Intelligence

## 1. Primary ICP: Mid-Market Commercial Enterprises & Accounting Teams
- **Company Size**: 20 - 500 employees.
- **Monthly Document Volume**: 500 - 5,000 invoices and receipts per month.
- **Key Target Industries**: Retail & E-commerce, Manufacturing, Wholesale Distribution, Construction, Professional Services, Logistics & 3PL.
- **Current Technology Stack**: QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage Intacct, NetSuite, Google Drive/Dropbox, Outlook/Gmail.

## 2. Target Personas

### Persona 1: Sarah — Lead Accounts Payable Specialist
- **Daily Burden**: Processes 80-120 vendor invoices daily. Manually checks PO numbers, types line items, and calculates taxes.
- **Key Frustration**: "OCR tools always drop line items or mess up decimals on multi-page invoices. If I have to double check every single field manually, why am I paying for software?"
- **Success Metric**: Auto-approve >70% of clean invoices with zero fear of math discrepancies; resolve remaining exceptions in <15 seconds.

### Persona 2: Marcus — Operations & Procurement Director
- **Daily Burden**: Manages 40+ vendor relationships, reconciles POs against invoices, and tracks project budgets.
- **Key Frustration**: "We lose early-payment 2% discounts because invoices take 2 weeks to get keyed and approved. Also, we had two cases last quarter where we paid duplicate invoices from the same supplier."
- **Success Metric**: Cut processing turnaround time from 14 days to under 4 hours; 100% duplicate detection before approval.
